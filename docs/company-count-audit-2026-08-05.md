## Count definitions

- Civic count is the current active non-inventory job count in the database.
- Official site count is the visible current-posting count on the company’s public job board.
- `+` difference means the official site shows more jobs than Civic Careers.
- Login-only, blocked, ambiguous, or non-comparable boards will be marked unverifiable rather than guessed.
